<objective>
Implement Daipai's server-side periodic aura damage using the final-level helper from Plan 01.

Purpose: The Buff must become a real dangerous gameplay effect before enchantment and later entity/item phases can build on it.
Output: Daipai ticks every 2 seconds, finds eligible living targets in range, and applies normal Minecraft damage attributed to the Buff holder.
</objective>

<scope>
In scope:
- Add the Daipai tick cadence to the status effect implementation.
- Calculate `finalLevel`, radius `finalLevel * 2`, and damage `finalLevel`.
- Damage all eligible living entities in range, including players.
- Exclude the aura owner and any Dongbei Yujie entities when such entity type information exists.
- Keep the exclusion future-proof if Phase 4's entity type does not exist yet.
- Use normal Minecraft damage handling.

Out of scope:
- Do not add enchantment acquisition or compatibility in this plan.
- Do not add custom particles, sounds, textures, or BGM in this plan; Plan 03 owns vanilla particle feedback.
- Do not add Big Sweaty Foot weapon, boot, or intimidation behavior.
</scope>
