<objective>
Implement the 2D Big Sweaty Foot visual hit particle. Register a custom particle type and its client-side sprite factory. Spawn it on both Daipai range damage and main-hand melee hits. Play the `daipai_trigger` sound.
</objective>

<scope>
In scope:
- Create `DongbeiYujieParticles.kt` to define and register `FOOT_HIT` (SimpleParticleType).
- Create `FootHitParticle.kt` on the client. It should extend `SpriteBillboardParticle`, be gravity-free, and last for 20 ticks (1s).
- Wire particle factory registration in `DongbeiYujieClient.kt`.
- Copy the item texture `big_sweaty_foot.png` to particle texture directory.
- Spawn the particle and play sounds in `DaipaiStatusEffect.applyUpdateEffect` and `BigSweatyFootItem.postHit`.

Out of scope:
- Do not add physical repulsion or knockback changes (already implemented in earlier phases).
</scope>
