Establish client-only and datagen/resource convention anchors while keeping them out of runtime metadata until later phases need them.

</objective>

<scope>
In scope:
- Keep client-only renderer and audio registration anchors under `src/client` per D-11 and D-12.
- Keep `DongbeiYujieClient.kt` as the future client orchestration location without requiring it in `fabric.mod.json` yet.
- Keep `DongbeiYujieDataGenerator.kt` as the future datagen convention anchor without registering providers or metadata entrypoints yet per D-17.
- Prepare resource/datagen naming conventions for `zh_cn`, `en_us`, item models/assets, entity resources, loot, sounds, and sound metadata per D-13 and D-16.

Out of scope:
- Do not register client renderers, client audio event handlers, data providers, real language JSON, model JSON, loot tables, `sounds.json`, PNG, OGG, WAV, MP3, BGM, or voice files in Phase 1 per D-14.
- Do not add image2-generated placeholder art; that is deferred to the later resource phase per D-15.
</scope>
